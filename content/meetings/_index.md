So you want to join us at HackWimbledon? As a rule of thumb we try to meet up on a couple of Sundays each month, but we have to be flexible depending on availability of Wimbletech's meeting space.

The specific pattern of meetings may vary, but you can always check on the events calendar. It is hosted on [lu.ma](https://luma.com/hackwimbledon)

Can't make it in person? We try to stream our sessions and hang out online, too.

Join us on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/py5VZarC2u) during the meeting times.
