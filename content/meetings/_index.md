# Meetups

Want to come along to a meetup? As a "rule of thumb" we try to meet up on a couple of Sundays each month, but this is flexible depending on the availability of Wimbletech's event space.

The specific pattern of meetings may vary, but you can always check on the events calendar. It is hosted on [lu.ma](https://luma.com/hackwimbledon).

## Where We Meet

We meet at **Wimbletech**, which has an entrance located behind Wimbledon Library along Compton Road.

{{< osmap lat="51.422219" lon="-0.208267" zoom="17" >}}

### Getting there

We are very close to Wimbledon Station (TfL District Line, trams, and mainline trains). There are good bus services to Wimbledon town centre. These are all just a couple of minutes walk from the venue. If you need it, parking is free along Compton Road on Sundays - although it can get very busy with other events locally. 

On meeting days, you will see HackWimbledon signs on the doors of Wimbletech Zone 2, please press the buzzer for entry.

## Remote Access

Can't make it in person? We try to stream our sessions and hang out online, too.

Join us on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/py5VZarC2u) during the meeting times.
