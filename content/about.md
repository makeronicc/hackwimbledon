All about HackWimbledon....

Way back in 2014, Dj was checking out the Wimbledon Library and wondering what was going on. Wimbletech was just setting up and after a chat about hacking and making and its synergy with small startups, we decided to start a popup hackspace with the newly created Wimbletech.

Fast forward to COVID and we created Makeroni, an idea for an umbrella group of online hackers who could meet under strict controls, which included HackWimbledon events. Fast forward again to 2025 and the need, at least for now, for Makeroni is gone and HackWimbledon is back and refreshed.
