Welcome to the HackWimbledon site. We're a popup hackspace in [Wimbledon, South West London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wimbledon,_London), where people gather to make, create and illuminate. Everything from home automation and robotics, to digitally enabled art and music. Check out the [About page](/about/) for more, find out [who comes to HackWimbledon and what we do](/peopleandprojects/) join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/py5VZarC2u), follow us on social media or just [come along](/meetings/) and enjoy creating.
