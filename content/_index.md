Welcome!

We're a pop-up hackspace in [Wimbledon, South West London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wimbledon,_London), where people gather to make, create, illuminate, and learn.

Everything from home automation and robotics, to digitally-enabled art and music. Hardware, software, making, and more.

Check out the [About page](/about/) to find out about us. You can also: learn about [who comes to our meetups, and our projects](/peopleandprojects/); join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/py5VZarC2u) to chat with us; follow us on [socials](https://mastodon.org.uk/@hackwimbledon); or, just [come along](/meetings/) and enjoy learning & creating!
