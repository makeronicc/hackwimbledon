Welcome to the HackWimbledon site. We're a popup hackspace in Wimbledon, South West London, where people gather to make, create and illuminate. Everything from home automation and robotics, to digitally enabled art and music. Check out the About page for more, find out [who we are and what we do](/peopleandprojects/) join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/HYYXHSu), follow us on social media or just [come along](/meetings/) and enjoy creating.
