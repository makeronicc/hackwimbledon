## Recently...

We've been visiting some interesting events lately.

In May, Barry was at Makers Central at the NEC, to meet some of his maker and tech heroes. Andy went to Swindon to check out Retrofest UK, which was a great event celebrating all things retro computing and gaming. It was fantastic to see so many people enjoying the nostalgia of old hardware and software.

## Electromagnetic Field

A couple of the HackWimbledon regulars (Andy and Barry, again) are heading to [Electromagnetic Field](https://emfcamp.org) next month. We don't have a specific plan yet, but we've marked our intended location on the site map as a starting point. It's more of a spot to park (near) than a formal village, although Andy's [Forge & Craft studio](https://forgeandcraft.co.uk) may have some activities going on during the camp.

Andy has dusted off his [Tildagon badge](https://tildagon.badge.emfcamp.org/), and is working on some HackWimbledon-related content for it... well, a logo app with QR code to our website, and some LED animation patterns. The fun starts now!
