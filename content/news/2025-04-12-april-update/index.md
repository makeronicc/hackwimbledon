Welcome to HackWimbledon. Rebooted. Let's get the important stuff out of the way. We're a pop-up hackspace which meets in real life every couple of weeks (and always online on Discord).

If you are interested in, or already do, coding, making, creating, crafting or something akin to that - where software and hardware are all part of your projects, or you just want to learn how to get into this "Making" stuff, this is where you can come.

We meetup around twice a month in Wimbledon at Wimbletech within the Library complex. We chat about making, projects, help each other out, give each other ideas, figure out solutions and teach folks how to do it themselves. When we say (make*, we mean everything from electronics, to assembling computers, to creating robots, to building home automation, to well, whatever we make it. You can find out when on the [calendar](/meetings).

If you want to get in touch by mail, drop a line to us at [hackwimbledon@gmail.com](mailto:hackwimbledon@gmail.com).

The whole thing is free (thanks to [Wimbletech](https://wimbletech.com/) for the space and WiFi)... so you have nothing to lose.
