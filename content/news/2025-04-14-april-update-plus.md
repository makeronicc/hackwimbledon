Yes, it's a new look for HackWimbledon with a refreshed Avatar, new look logo and a favourite for [Dosis](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Dosis) being used across the site. The old logo was a fine use of good old Lobsters, but it was looking a bit old, especially to the folks who get to see it most days. The new avatar is Dosis too but this logo uses the initials of HackWimbledon to bloom into life. It also means that this:

![Big HackWimbledon](/img/hwlogo2025sitetitle.svg)

.... is now the new look for HackWimbledon.

There's various bits happening on the site, so scuse our dust while we finish up an About page and populate the People and Projects section. It's always a work in progress.

But the big thing is meeting up. And with that in mind, here's a biggie. Hack on the Common. We'll be spending a day on battery, on lorawan and in touch all over the common in May (weather permitting of course) so look out for the big announcment on that, coming soon.
